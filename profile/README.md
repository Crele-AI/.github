# Crele
 
**Audio AI that works in the real world.**
 
Most audio AI is built for clean input. Real recordings have a second person talking, a TV on in the background, a café table too close. Crele works on those.
 
## What we work on
 
Speaker-aware audio — separating, cleaning and attributing speech when more than one person is in the room.
 
- **Target speaker extraction.** Given a few seconds of one person's voice, isolate that person and remove the others. This is not noise reduction, and it is the thing noise reduction cannot do: a second voice is signal, not noise.
- **Speech enhancement that doesn't rewrite the voice.** Cleanup without the generative artefacts that make "enhanced" audio sound robotic.
- **Speaker-conditioned front ends for voice agents.** So an agent responds to its user and not to the television, the bystander, or the drive-thru next lane over.
